# SOURCES — append-only

Every primary source this curriculum cites: **title, identifier, URL, the date the record was
checked, and the parts citing it.**

Three rules, from plan §5 and §20.4.1:

1. **Looked up live** on the day the part is written. A remembered citation is an invented citation.
2. **Cited by title and identifier, never by author** (§21.1 rule 5).
3. **Taught once in the whole curriculum.** The day that first needs a source carries its source
   part; every later day cites it and links there.
